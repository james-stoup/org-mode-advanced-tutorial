* Pulled Pork BBQ
** The best kind of bbq
- don't let Texans fool you
- brisket is nice
- but properly smoked pork is better
** The sauce is key
- mustard based sauce is the best
- followed by NC style vinegar sauces
- don't let a tomato near your pork
** How to make the best sauce
*** Ingredients
- 1 cup yellow mustard
- 1/2 cup granulated sugar
- 1/3 cup light brown sugar
- 3/4 cup cider vinegar
- 1/4 cup water
- 2 tablespoons mild chili powder
- 1 teaspoon freshly ground black pepper
- 1 teaspoon freshly ground white pepper
- 1/2 teaspoon cayenne pepper
- 1 teaspoon soy sauce
- 2 tablespoons unsalted butter
- 1 tablespoon liquid smoke, hickory flavor
*** Directions
1. Combine all the ingredients, except soy sauce, butter, and liquid smoke
2. Simmer on low for 20 minutes, making sure to stir occasionally.
3. Add the soy sauce, butter, and liquid smoke and simmer for 10 more minutes.
4. Remove the sauce from heat and allow the mixture to cool
